offEarth

AI that works with the Wi-Fi off.

What it is.

offEarth runs a language model on the device itself. No account, no server, nothing leaves your phone. After the first model download it works anywhere, including the places with no signal.

What I did

I design and build the apps and the on-device AI underneath them.

In daily use on iOS and macOS. Not on the App Store yet.

What went wrong

A model download failed quietly and saved a 15-byte error message as if it were a 4.7 GB model. Tap send, white screen. Now every model file has to prove what it is before the app will load it.

01

It checks your phone first

It reads how much memory the device has and recommends the biggest model that will run cleanly, from 1.1 GB up to 4.7 GB.

02

It backs off before iOS makes it

It watches memory, heat and battery mode while you chat, and trims what the model is holding before the system shuts the app down.

03

The browser can’t phone home

The Mac browser’s assistant only talks to a local address. Its networking is compiled without the ability to reach the public internet.
