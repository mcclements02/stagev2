Sango Sports Lab

Film one rep. Get told the truth about it.

What it is.

Sango turns a phone video of a single rep into a graded report. A jump, a sprint, a swing, a throw. It tracks 17 joints on the phone, scores six things about how you moved, and gives you drills for next time. It covers 16 sports and it’s on the App Store.

What I did

All of it. The iOS app, the web portal coaches use, the cloud functions, the billing, and the App Store submission.

What went wrong

The AI coach started giving every athlete the same answer, word for word. The model was spending nearly its whole budget thinking and had about 20 tokens left to talk. I raised one limit and it started coaching again.

01

The AI doesn’t measure anything

The phone measures the movement first. The language model only explains the numbers. If the two disagree, the measurement wins.

02

Blank beats made up

One camera can’t see everything. Each sport has a list of things it isn’t allowed to score, and those come back empty.

03

The same math, twice

The scoring engine runs in Swift on the phone and in TypeScript in the cloud. Tests keep the two from drifting apart.
